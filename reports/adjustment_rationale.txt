We computed baseline first/second line xG/60 using total TOI, then added an opponent-defense adjustment for interpretability.
Specifically, we split each team’s first_off and second_off production by the opponent’s defense pairing (first_def vs second_def).
We upweighted production against first_def by 1.10 (vs 1.00 for second_def), then recomputed xG/60 using weighted xG and weighted TOI.
This keeps the metric comparable across teams while acknowledging that not all minutes are equally difficult due to matchup quality.
We report both baseline and adjusted top-10 lists and quantify rank shifts to make the impact of the adjustment transparent.
